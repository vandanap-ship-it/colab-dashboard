# Siddhi — Site Engineer Cheat Sheet

**Who this is for:** every site engineer on Amanvana Phase 1.
**When to use it:** end of shift, every working day.
**Time it takes:** 2 minutes per villa you worked on today.

---

## Step 1 — Open Siddhi on your phone

- Open the app icon on your home screen (labelled **Siddhi**).
- If you don't see the icon yet: open Safari / Chrome, go to `siddhi-whitelotus.vercel.app`, then:
  - **Android Chrome:** tap the ⋮ menu → **Add to Home Screen**
  - **iOS Safari:** tap the share icon → **Add to Home Screen**

## Step 2 — Sign in (only the first time)

- Type your username and password given to you by the office.
- Tick **Keep me signed in**.

## Step 3 — Home screen has 3 buttons

| Button | When to tap |
|---|---|
| **Add Progress** | For every activity you moved today |
| **Manpower** | Once per day — how many workers came in |
| **DLR** | Once per day — end-of-day site summary |

---

## Add Progress — 5 taps

1. **Pick the villa** you worked on (V03, V17, etc.).
2. **Pick the activity** (Retaining Wall PCC, Column Reinforcement, etc.). The list shows only activities that are currently open for that villa.
3. **Set the % complete** using the slider or type the number.
4. **Tap the camera icon** to attach a photo. You can add up to 4 photos per entry. Take a fresh one — old photos from your gallery are fine too.
5. If the activity is delayed: **pick a Delay Reason** (Materials, Manpower, Change orders, etc.). Add a short remark if useful.
6. Tap **Save**.

You'll see a green tick — the entry is logged. Repeat for every activity you touched.

---

## Manpower — 30 seconds

- Enter today's headcount per trade (Bar Bender, Carpenter, Helper, Mason).
- Tap **Save**.

If the site was closed for a holiday, tick **Site Closed** and save — Siddhi won't count that day against your plan.

---

## DLR (Daily Log Report)

- Add photos of the site condition at end of shift.
- Note anything unusual — a hindrance, a visitor, an inspection.
- Tap **Save**.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **"Failed to save" red message** → your phone lost signal. The entry is saved locally and will upload automatically when signal returns. Don't re-enter it.
- **App won't open** → open browser, go to `siddhi-whitelotus.vercel.app`. If that works, the home-screen icon needs a re-install (see Step 1).
- **Forgot your password** → call the office. Don't share your login with anyone else.

Siddhi replaces Colab Tools. It's where you log site progress, raise work permits, and track daily site records. Below is everything you need for day-to-day use.

## Two views

Siddhi shows you a different home page depending on whether you're on your **phone** or **laptop**:

- **On your phone** — home page has big tap-tiles for daily actions (Log Progress, Raise Work Permit, Log Hindrance).
- **On your laptop** — home page shows the project dashboard (schedule, delay, milestones). Click **Menu** (top-right) for all tools.

Both views use the same data — anything you do on the phone shows up on the laptop, and vice versa.

---

## The four daily flows

### 1. Log daily progress (site staff, on phone)

Home → **New Progress** → pick the activity → enter today's quantity → **Save.**

If you're offline, it queues and sends when signal comes back.

### 2. Raise a work permit

**On phone:** Home → **Raise Work Permit**
**On laptop:** Menu → **Work Permits** → **Raise Work Permit** (top-right button)

Fill in: type (Hot Work / Night Work / De-shuttering / General), date, times, location, contractor, and **who should approve**. Submit.

The approver gets an email. You'll see the permit in **My Requests** with a yellow "AWAITING APPROVAL" chip. Once approved, chip turns green. At end of day, come back and tap **Close**.

### 3. Approve a work permit (planners, managers)

You'll get an email when someone assigns you as approver. Or check **Work Permits → My Approvals** any time — anything pending shows there.

Tap the row → **Approve** or **Reject** (rejecting needs a reason).

### 4. Log a hindrance (anything blocking your work)

Home → **Log Hindrance** → pick the activity affected → describe what's blocking → attach photos → **Save.**

The planner sees it in the desktop Hindrance list.

## Where the templates live

**Phone:** bottom nav → **Documents** → scroll to Permits / Checklists / Records.

Tap any template to download. Standard formats for permit forms, safety checklists, records.

If you need a new template added, tell Shraddha.

## Switching between projects

Top of every page: **"Switch Project"** button (next to the Siddhi logo). Click it to see all projects you have access to and jump between them.
